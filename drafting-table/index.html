Features

From 3D model to measured drawing

Import an STL, OBJ or USDZ file and get a measured 2D line drawing. It's how the app's own icon was made: a drawing, by the app, of a drafting table.

Trace over anything

Bring in a PDF or image as an underlay, then calibrate it with two clicks and one real-world distance. Everything you draw and measure on top of it is true to scale.

Draw, dimension, annotate

Lines, dimensions, annotations, tables and scale bars, with snapping to keep it tidy. What the sheet says is what the thing measures.

Print at exact scale

Sheets export as true-vector PDF or PNG, so a drawing marked 1:10 comes off the printer at 1:10. Take it to the shop and put a ruler on it.

Nine starter templates

Title blocks, site plan, shop drawing, cut list, assembly and parts list, cover sheet and detail sheet. Start from a real sheet instead of a blank page.

Local-first, no strings

Documents are ordinary .plansheet files on your disk. No account, no cloud, no subscription. Buy it once on the Mac App Store and it's yours.
