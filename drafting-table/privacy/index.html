Drafting Table Privacy Policy

Effective July 24, 2026

The short version: Drafting Table collects no data. Not "minimal data" — none.

What the app collects

Nothing. Drafting Table has no analytics, no telemetry, and no crash reporting service. It makes no network connections. There is no account to create, and the app never asks who you are.

Your documents

Your plan sheets are ordinary .plansheet files saved wherever you choose on your Mac. They never leave your computer unless you move them yourself. I have no way to see them, and no copy of them exists anywhere else.

Crash reports

If the app crashes, you may choose to email me a crash report — technical details about what went wrong — using your own mail app. Nothing is ever sent automatically, and you can read exactly what's in the email before deciding to send it. If you do send one, I use it only to fix the bug.

Purchases

Drafting Table is sold through the Mac App Store. Apple handles the purchase and all payment information; I never see your payment details. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

This website

None of the above involves this website, but for completeness: justice.engineering is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may log visitor IP addresses as part of operating the service, as described in the GitHub Pages documentation. The site itself sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Pages load the Raleway typeface from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests the font files from Google's servers when the page loads.

Changes to this policy

If any of the above ever changes — for example, if a future version of the app gains a feature that needs the network — this policy will be updated before that version ships, with a new effective date at the top.

Contact

Questions about privacy, or anything else: work@justice.engineering.
