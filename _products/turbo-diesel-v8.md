A photo-led exterior of a turbo diesel V8, built for Blender visualization, with a separate engine-neutral glTF version for real-time use. The detailed edition's GLB preview is simplified and won't match the procedural Blender shaders; the runtime edition is a separately baked set.

## Not included

Transmission, internals, vehicle plumbing and the surrounding vehicle. No automatic LOD switcher, animation or collision hulls.

## Know before you buy

This is a proportional, photo-led exterior. The donor and accessory options, belt arrangement, dimensions and concealed routes are not verified. It is not fitment CAD, a print-ready solid, a service reference or an exact replica. The runtime LOD1 and LOD2 use geometry normals rather than normal maps. Tested in Blender 5.1.2; no target-engine frame rate is promised.

## About the files

Procedural geometry and material development in Blender, with automated checks and render review against reference photographs. No specialist certification is claimed. Every image and the turntable are renders of this version.

Ford and Power Stroke identify the reference subject. This independent model is not endorsed by Ford or Garrett, and no logos or serial numbers are reproduced.
