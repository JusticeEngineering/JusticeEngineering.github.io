Includes the clutch and ignition cases, cylinder and head, reed inlet and PJ-family carburetor, gear-driven water pump, transfer hose, output sprocket and guard, kickstart, shifter, spark plug and short engine-side breather and electrical stubs.

## Not included

Frame and mounting brackets, radiators and vehicle hoses, expansion chamber and silencer, airbox, chain, frame electronics and harness, and engine internals. No animation or rig.

## Know before you buy

The service manual's 89 mm bore and 79 mm stroke are engine specs; the internals are not modeled. Overall size, mount centers, clearances and the hidden rear and underside are inferred from manual views, parts drawings and photos. The modeled 38 mm carburetor venturi and 7-degree cylinder inclination were checked within stated tolerances. The carburetor is a PJ-family interpretation; the PJ27P and PJ27N sources conflict, and no stamp or jetting is claimed. The finish is a clean-service interpretation, and the lettering uses original approximate glyphs. LOD2 omits the raised lettering, and lower LODs show coarser curves and small shading marks up close. Not measured CAD, a fitment model or print-ready. Performance was measured in Blender's EEVEE viewport on one Apple M2 Mac.

## About the files

Built in Blender with Python, using original procedural meshes and materials. No third-party meshes, scans, fonts or photo textures. Every image and the turntable show the supplied model.
